An unforgettable week at the CIV Summer School by the beautiful Lake Geneva in Switzerland!

## Presenting My Research

I had the exciting opportunity to present my first poster [**Blockage-aware Multi-armed Bandits for Proactive Handover in mmWave Vehicular Networks**](https://chiqq3.github.io/assets/pdf/chi2025civ_poster.pdf) at the poster session. It was amazing to discuss my work with fellow researchers and exchange ideas in such an inspiring setting!
