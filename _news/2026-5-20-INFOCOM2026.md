An incredible experience at one of the most prestigious networking conferences in the world!

## Volunteering at INFOCOM 2026

I had the wonderful opportunity to serve as a **Student Volunteer** at [**IEEE INFOCOM 2026**](https://infocom2026.ieee-infocom.org/), held in **Tokyo, Japan**. INFOCOM is one of the top-tier international conferences on computer communications and networking, attracting leading researchers and engineers from around the globe each year.

Beyond general volunteering, I also took on the role of **cameraman**, capturing keynote speeches, panel sessions, technical sessions, and memorable moments throughout the conference. It was a unique behind-the-lens perspective that gave me a front-row seat to cutting-edge research being presented live!

## Reflections

Meeting so many brilliant minds, from seasoned professors to fellow PhD students, reminded me why I love this field. Serving behind the camera also taught me to pay closer attention to how ideas are communicated, not just what is being said. Oh, and the exclusive **INFOCOM 2026 volunteer T-shirt** was definitely a crowd favorite. More than a few people came up asking where they could get one! 😄 I left feeling genuinely inspired and more motivated than ever to push forward with my own research!
