Today marks a special milestone—my personal logo has officially come to life!

## The Story Behind the Design

The inspiration comes from **Mayohachi**, a little rescue kitten I found on the streets of Shenzhen, China in 2023 when she was just one year old. Combined with WiFi signal waves representing my research passion in wireless communications, this logo perfectly captures who I am: a researcher who loves both cats and connectivity.

## Design Journey

The creation process was a delightful adventure following three steps:

**Mayohachi → Hand-drawn Watercolor → Digital Edition**

Starting from candid photos of Mayohachi, I sketched her characteristic curious expression in watercolor, then transformed it into a clean digital logo. The WiFi waves naturally flow around her silhouette, symbolizing how wireless signals invisibly connect our world—just like how this little cat connected with my heart.
