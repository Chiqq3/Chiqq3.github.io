Thrilled to have presented our latest research at one of the leading venues for vehicular networking!

## Presentation at IEEE VNC 2026

I had the honor of presenting our paper **"Fast-Adapting V2X Networks: A Dynamic Context Clustering Multi-Armed Bandits"** at [**IEEE Vehicular Networking Conference (VNC) 2026**](https://www.ieeevnc.org/), held in **Montreal, Canada**.

The work introduces **DCC-SMAB**, a Dynamic Context Clustering Semi-distributed Multi-Armed Bandit algorithm for user association in mmWave vehicular networks. By dynamically clustering vehicle positions as contexts, the algorithm achieves rapid adaptation to non-stationary communication environments, demonstrating over **61% regret reduction** and **76% transmission rate improvement** compared to UCB baselines.
