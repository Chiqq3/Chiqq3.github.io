The wait is finally over — my internship PR video from the **Ehime Prefectural Industrial Technology Research Institute** has officially gone public! 🎉

## About the Internship

Last August, I spent an exciting week in Ehime developing **Mikan Hunt**, an augmented reality game built with HoloLens 2 where players pick virtual oranges in mixed reality — a fun nod to Ehime's most iconic fruit! 🍊

You can check out the full project details [here](/projects/mikan_intern.md).
