An incredible week at IEEE VTC2024-Spring in beautiful Singapore – my first international conference experience!

## Presenting My Research

I had the honor of presenting my paper [**V2I Blockage Modeling and Performance Evaluation for Autonomous Driving**](https://chiqq3.github.io/assets/pdf/chi2024v2i.pdf) at the conference. The research addresses critical challenges in vehicle-to-infrastructure communication using mmWave technology, focusing on RSU-assisted systems to tackle blockages in multi-lane scenarios.

## Young Researcher's Encouragement Award

I'm thrilled to have received the **IEEE VTS Tokyo/Japan Chapter Young Researcher's Encouragement Award**!
